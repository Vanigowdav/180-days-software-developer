Veg Fried Rice

A quick and flavorful Indo-Chinese style fried rice made with leftover rice, mixed vegetables, and simple spices.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice(preferably a day old)
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables(carrot, beans, capsicum, peas)
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon vinegar
  • Salt to taste
  • Pepper to taste
  • Spring onion for garnish

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan on high flame
  2. Add chopped garlic and onion, saute for a mixture.
  3. Add mixed vegetables and stir fry for 3-4 minutes.
  4. Add cooked rice and mix well.
  5. Pour soy sauce and vinegar, then add salt and pepper.
  6. Stir everything together for 2-3 minutes on high flame.
  7. Garnish with spring onion and serve hot.

Enjoy your homemade fried rice - best served hot with a side of manchurian or chilli sauce.
