Simple Veg Pulao

A quick, one-pot rice dish loaded with vegetables

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, peas, beans)
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tsp pulao masala
  • 3 cups water
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the rice for 20 minutes.
  2. Saute the onions until golden.
  3. Add vegetables and pulao masala, cook for 3 minutes.
  4. Add rice, water and salt. Cover and cook until done.
