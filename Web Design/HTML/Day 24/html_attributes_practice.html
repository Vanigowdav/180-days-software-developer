How to make a tea

Ingredients

Water, Milk, Tea powder, Sugar, Ginger

Preparation Time

This tea takes about 10 minutes to prepare

Steps

Step 1: Boil water

Step 2: Add tea powder

Step 3: Add milk and sugar

Step 4: Simmer

Step 5: Strain and serve

Tips

For a stronger flavor, let it simmer a bit longer.
